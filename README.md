# Customer Retention & Profitability Analysis

## Overview
Multi-page interactive dashboard analyzing 10,000+ retail transactions with focus on customer retention, geographic distribution, and product profitability.

## Live Dashboard
[View Dashboard](https://datastudio.google.com/reporting/28aca29c-1a58-48c4-9f7a-b7015bee087a)

## Pages
- **Executive Overview** — KPIs, geographic map, top products by profit
- **Sales Trends Analysis** — Sales over time, category trends by year
- **Customer Segment Analysis** — Segment breakdown, top customers
- **Product Analysis** — Top products, sub-category distribution, discount analysis

## Tools Used
- Excel — data cleaning
- Google Looker Studio — multi-page visualization

## Key Insights
- Consumer segment represents 50.6% of total sales
- California and New York are the highest revenue states
- Technology leads in sales across all years
- Some products show high discounts with negative profit impact

[Superstore Sales — Kaggle](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/ishanshrivastava28/superstore-sales)
